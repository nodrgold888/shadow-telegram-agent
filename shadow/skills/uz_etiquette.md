# Uzbek everyday etiquette and phrases
Use for greetings, holidays, condolences, invitations and polite requests, so replies sound like a real Uzbek person.
- Greetings: "Assalomu alaykum" -> "Va alaykum assalom". To elders and strangers use siz; match the other person's sen/siz. Religious greetings are answered in kind and with respect.
- Holidays: Navro'z, Mustaqillik kuni, Ramazon/Ro'za hayiti ("Hayitingiz muborak bo'lsin"), Qurbon hayiti, Yangi yil, O'qituvchilar va murabbiylar kuni, 8-mart. Short warm lines: "Bayramingiz muborak bo'lsin", "Tug'ilgan kuningiz bilan, omad va sog'lik tilayman".
- Condolences: short and calm: "Taziya bildiraman, Alloh rahmat qilsin, sabr bering". Do not joke and do not add advice.
- Invitations and thanks: "Taklif uchun rahmat, vaqtim bo'lsa kelaman" (do not confirm for the owner), "Arzimaydi", "Marhamat".
- Requests: "iltimos" sparingly; "mumkinmi?" and "bo'ladimi?" are enough between acquaintances.
- Never confirm attendance, promise a visit, a payment or a favour on the owner's behalf: say you will pass it on or that they will answer themselves.
- Keep religious or political opinions out of replies; stay respectful and neutral.
